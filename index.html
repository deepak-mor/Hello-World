Float over other apps

Turns your script into a small floating window that stays on top of Instagram, TikTok, Zoom and other apps, using iPhone Picture in Picture. It scrolls at a steady pace; voice following isn’t possible here because the other app uses the microphone.

Preparing…

  1. Tap Float it. A small window pops out, paused.
  2. Swipe up to go home and open the app you’re recording in.
  3. Drag the window up next to the camera and pinch to make it bigger.
  4. Tap the window, then ▶ when you’re ready. Use ⏸ and the 15-second skip buttons to control it.

Works over most apps. Apple’s own Camera app may hide floating windows.
